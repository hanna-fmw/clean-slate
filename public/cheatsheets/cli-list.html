cli-list

Inventory of every CLI installed in your user-controlled bin dirs.

cli-listFull grouped list (your scripts, cargo, brew, /usr/local)
cli-list --personalOnly your own scripts in ~/.local/bin and ~/bin
cli-list --brewOnly Homebrew (/opt/homebrew/bin)
cli-list --search XFilter CLIs by substring
cli-list <name>Run that tool's --help (falls back to -h)

Lives at ~/.local/bin/cli-list.
